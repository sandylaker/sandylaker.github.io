I received my Ph.D. from the [Statistical Learning and Data Science (SLDS)](https://www.slds.stat.uni-muenchen.de)
group at _LMU Munich_, where I was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Bernd Bischl](https://www.slds.stat.uni-muenchen.de/people/bischl/)
and was a junior member of the [Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML)](https://mcml.ai).
In 2024, I was a visiting researcher at the lab of
[Prof. Dr. Kenji Kawaguchi](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/kenji/) at
the _National University of Singapore (NUS)_.

My research aims to make LLMs more reliable and efficient.
I work on **calibration of LLMs**, so that a model's confidence reflects how likely it is
to be correct; **post-training of large reasoning models**, such as self-distillation; and
**efficient LLMs**, from pruning model layers to making reasoning more concise.

My journey started in China, where I earned a bachelor's degree in Laboratory
Medical Science from _Southern Medical University_ in Guangzhou. My passion for engineering
then brought me to Germany, where I completed a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at
_TU Darmstadt_. Intrigued by the intersection of technology and medicine, I went on to
an M.Sc. in Biomedical Computing at the _Technical University of Munich (TUM)_.
